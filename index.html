About Chuka University Hostel Hub

The Chuka University Hostel Hub is the official digital platform for student accommodation management. Our platform is directly connected to the university's database, showing real-time availability of all registered hostels around Chuka University.

How it works:

  • ✅ Hostel owners register through the admin panel
  • ✅ Real hostel data is fetched from Django backend
  • ✅ Students see actual availability and prices
  • ✅ Online booking system connected to real database
  • ✅ 24/7 updated information

Note: All hostels displayed are registered with Chuka University and meet the required accommodation standards.
